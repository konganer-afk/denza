Citation & Formula Key
  • [P1]Fuel price default — ABS/DISER national average (2024). User-editable.
  • [P2]Electricity price default — AEMO national average (2024). User-editable.
  • [C1]ICE Annual Cost = Annual km × (L/100km ÷ 100) × Fuel price per litre
  • [C2]Annual Distance = Daily commute km × Days/week × 52 weeks
  • [S1] EVDENZA Annual Cost = (Annual km ÷ 100) × kWh/100km × Electricity price
  • [S1] PHEVDENZA Annual Cost = Annual km × (L/100km ÷ 100 × Fuel price + Wh/km ÷ 1000 × Electricity price)
  • [S2]Monthly Saving = Annual Saving ÷ 12
  • [V1]% Saving = (Annual Saving ÷ ICE Annual Cost) × 100
  • [I1]Average Large SUV — 8.9 L/100km — Green Vehicle Guide (Diesel, 3.3L 6cyl Turbo, 10-spd Auto, 4WD)
  • [I2]Average Mid-Size SUV — 7.6 L/100km — Green Vehicle Guide (Diesel, 2.8L 4cyl Turbo, 8-spd Auto, 4WD)
  • [I3]Average Luxury SUV — 9.9 L/100km — Green Vehicle Guide (Petrol 95RON, 3.0L 6cyl Turbo, 8-spd Auto, 4WD)
  • [I4]Average Large MPV — 9.6 L/100km — Green Vehicle Guide (Petrol 91RON, 3.5L 6cyl, 8-spd Auto)
  • [D1]Denza D9 AWD — 25.6 kWh/100km — GVG / MY26 spec sheet (derived: 103.36 kWh ÷ 480 km WLTC × 1000). Pure EV.
  • [D2]Denza B5 — 1.9 L/100km + 233 Wh/km — GVG / MY26 spec sheet (PHEV combined, SOC 25%–100%)
  • [D3]Denza B8 — 2.0 L/100km + 244 Wh/km — GVG / MY26 spec sheet (PHEV combined, SOC 25%–100%)
